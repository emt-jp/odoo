🚗 Car Rental Fleet Management

A comprehensive fleet management solution for car rental businesses, featuring advanced booking management, vehicle tracking, maintenance scheduling, and analytics reporting with modern UI/UX design.

✨ Key Features

📊 Fleet Dashboard

Real-time KPI monitoring with visual indicators for vehicle availability, utilization rates, and operational metrics.

📅 Booking Management

Intuitive booking system with status badges, kanban views, and streamlined workflow from reservation to completion.

🔧 Maintenance Tracking

Comprehensive maintenance management with progress indicators, cost breakdown, and automated scheduling.

📄 Document Management

Automated document expiration tracking with color-coded countdown badges and proactive alerts.

💰 Pricing & Revenue

Transparent pricing breakdowns, flexible discount management, and comprehensive revenue analytics.

📈 Analytics & Reports

Detailed reporting on fleet performance, rental trends, revenue analysis, and operational efficiency.

🎨 Modern UI/UX Design

Status Badges Color-Coded Indicators Kanban Dashboards Progress Tracking Visual Alerts Responsive Layout

🔗 Integration

Seamlessly integrates with HR Fleet Integration and HR Payroll Custom modules for comprehensive driver management and payroll processing.
